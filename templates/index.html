Diet / media

What should a diet file look like?

A plain-text table (CSV, TSV or semicolon-separated) with one row per nutrient. A header row is optional (it is detected and skipped). There are two layouts:

  • Two columns — id, flux. A positive number is the maximum uptake rate (mmol/gDW/h), so the exchange's lower bound becomes its negative. The upper (secretion) bound stays as in the model.
  • Three columns — id, lower bound, upper bound. Both bounds are used exactly as given, e.g. -10,1000 allows uptake up to 10 and free secretion; -10,-10 forces uptake of exactly 10.
  • The id can be an exchange reaction (EX_glc__D_e) or a metabolite (glc__D, glc__D[e]). Rows that match no exchange in the model are listed after the analysis.
  • Every exchange not listed is closed for uptake, so list everything the cells may consume.
